across the series and their Lean~4 formalization. Nothing here is

reached their final form.

\section{L2: from prose to proof}

\paragraph{Original statement (series papers 04 and 08).}
\emph{``In any interval with no progress, convertible stock is monotone
non-increasing.''} The prose carried a parenthetical---``in the
no-injection regime that holds once a set is blocked''---doing
load-bearing work without being part of the claim.

\paragraph{The counterexample the machine forced.} A no-progress
interval may contain a \emph{flow} step: reservoir shares injected into
a runnable process that then fails to convert. Convertible stock rises;
no progress is made. The unrestricted statement is false.

\paragraph{Corrected statement (proven).}
\begin{law}[L2, final form]
Along any reachable sequence of non-injecting transitions (drain and
yield steps), convertible stock is monotone non-increasing. Moreover,
each \emph{blocked} process's stock is individually non-increasing
under arbitrary mixed sequences, and flow steps leave blocked stocks
unchanged.
\end{law}

\paragraph{Why nothing downstream weakens.} Detection reads blocked
processes; their stocks are monotone unconditionally
(\texttt{blocked\_stock\_monotone} in \texttt{Reachable.lean}), so
detection soundness (\texttt{detection\_sound}) is untouched.

\paragraph{The lesson, recorded.} The parenthetical was the hypothesis.
Formalization did not merely check the theory; it falsified one clause
and returned the repaired clause with a proof---the epistemic invariant
applied to the theory itself: let the ledger overrule the narrative.
